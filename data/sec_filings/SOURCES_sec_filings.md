## Mismatches (see processed/lcc_quarters_check.csv, match = NO or yes (basis))
1. **Allegiant ASM basis is mixed** in frontier/raw/allegiant_quarterly_ops_2018Q3_2026Q3.csv: `asm_m` is TOTAL-SYSTEM ASM in 19 of the 23 checked quarters (2018Q3, 2018Q4, 2019Q3, 2019Q4, 2022Q1–2025Q1, 2025Q3, 2026Q1) but SCHEDULED-service ASM in 2025Q2, 2025Q4, 2026Q2, 2026Q3. RPM and load factor are scheduled-service everywhere.
   - Effect on lcc_quarters.csv: Allegiant 2025Q2 `seats` = 12.28 compares scheduled 2025Q2 (5,629.04) to total 2024Q2 (5,013.21). Consistent values: 15.68 (total) or 16.11 (scheduled). All other Allegiant `seats` values are consistent on a total-system basis (scheduled basis differs by 0.2–0.9 pp).
   - Effect on Thesis 1 regression (e1_sensitivity.expected.out): seats-fixed fare coefficient −0.159 → −0.155 (2025Q2 fixed) or −0.154 (all scheduled); R² 0.841 → 0.844. Deck's −0.16 / 0.84 still holds after rounding.
2. **Allegiant load factor 2018Q3, 2018Q4, 2019Q3**: our 82.03 / 78.95 / 81.54 are RPM ÷ total-system ASM; reported scheduled LF is 85.7 / 82.3 / 86.0. LF is not used by build_lcc_quarters.py.
3. **Sun Country `asm_total_m` column** (not used by the build script, which uses RPM ÷ LF): 2023Q1, 2023Q2, 2023Q4, 2025Q4 hold SCHEDULED ASM, not total; 2022Q4 is 1,645.66 vs reported total 1,657.21 (scheduled 1,352.83) — a typo.
4. Route launch dates: BNA–TPA (Oct 9) and ONT–OAK (Nov 21) not found in any Frontier press release (see route_launch_dates.csv).
5. Fleet: A320ceo seats 186 in fleet_seats.csv; filings give "180 or 186" / "180–186".

Everything else checked matches within rounding: all Frontier 2022Q1–2026Q3 RPM, ASM, LF, passenger revenue, passengers, fare revenue (fare revenue derived as fare/passenger × passengers, ±$1M); all Allegiant RPM, passengers (total system), passenger revenue, total average fare; all Sun Country RPM, LF, passenger revenue; every fare/traffic value in lcc_quarters.csv and all Frontier/Sun Country seats values; fleet Dec '23, Dec '24, Dec '25, Sep '26; Q2 2026 traffic; LAS–OAK, LAS–BOI, DEN–FLL, MCO–CTG dates.

## Not checked
- Frontier 2019Q1–2021Q4 and Allegiant 2019Q1–Q2, 2020Q1–2021Q4 (COVID / no prior-year base: not used in lcc_quarters.csv). Marked `not checked` in the csv.
- Allegiant 2026Q2–Q3 include Sun Country from May 13, 2026 (acquisition); fine for the panel, but they are not like-for-like with 2025.
- Method notes (not errors): Sun Country passenger revenue includes charter and ancillary while RPM is scheduled-only, so its "fare" (yield) mixes bases; Frontier fare_revenue has no quarterly income-statement line (derived from per-passenger fare).
