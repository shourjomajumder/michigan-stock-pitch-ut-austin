# Agent 05: Wi-Fi / Starlink / seatback screens and airline choice

Searches used: 4 of 5.

## A. Stated-preference surveys (upper bounds; hypothetical)

1. Viasat Passenger Experience Survey 2023 (Censuswide, n=11,053, fieldwork July 2023)
   URL: https://www.viasat.com/content/dam/us-site/aviation/perspectives/documents/Passenger_Experience_Survey_2023.pdf
   Also Runway Girl Network, 19 Sep 2023: https://runwaygirlnetwork.com/2023/09/free-in-flight-wi-fi-most-influential-viasat-survey
   - "free Wi-Fi is the most influential factor besides ticket price, with 22% of respondents selecting this option."
   - "83% of passengers surveyed would rebook with an airline if they offered a quality Wi-Fi experience."
   - "3 in 10 passengers surveyed (29%) are willing to pay more for their ticket to benefit from free Wi-Fi."
   - US: demand for free Wi-Fi "50% up from 2022."

2. Inmarsat Inflight Connectivity Survey (3rd annual, n=9,000, 18 countries, fieldwork Feb 2017)
   APEX, 26 Jul 2017: https://apex.aero/articles/44-pax-stop-flying-preferred-airline-due-poor-connectivity
   - "44 percent of passengers would stop using their preferred airline in the next year" if it offered poor in-flight connectivity.
   PhocusWire: https://www.phocuswire.com/Wifi-now-in-top-three-factors-making-passengers-switch-airline
   - "nearly half (44%) of all respondents would switch airlines if wifi was not available onboard"

3. Viasat survey as cited by Live and Let's Fly, 9 Aug 2026 (survey of more than 11,000):
   https://liveandletsfly.com/united-virgin-atlantic-prove-starlink-wi-fi-is-winning/
   - "75% are more likely to choose an airline offering quality inflight Wi-Fi"

## B. United Starlink revealed outcomes (satisfaction only; no share or NPS numbers published)

- SpaceX case study (undated): Wi-Fi CSAT "2X"; 7M+ passengers, 3.7M+ devices, 167K flights, 344 aircraft; target "three out of every four departures" by end 2026.
  https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/Case%20study%20%28done%29/connectivity%20business%201_United.pdf
- United press release, 2 Feb 2026: Wi-Fi satisfaction on Starlink aircraft "nearly doubled"; 300+ regional aircraft; more than 25% of daily departures (about 1,200 flights); 500+ mainline by end 2026.
  https://united.mediaroom.com/2026-02-02-United-Spotlights-Starlink-Wi-Fi-in-New-Big-Game-Ad-as-Airline-Completes-Installation-on-300-Regional-Aircraft
- Customer Experience Dive, 16 Jul 2026 (Q2 2026 earnings): Wi-Fi CSAT "more than double the scores of other Wi-Fi operating aircraft"; Starlink on 400 aircraft, "nearly 1,000 aircraft by the end of the year"; "loyalty revenue grew 11% year over year"; Bay Area best share-gain hub, "3.4 percentage points" YoY in Q1 (not attributed to Wi-Fi).
  https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/united-airlines-ceo-brand-loyalty-wins/825473/
- Virgin Atlantic (via Live and Let's Fly, 9 Aug 2026): connectivity CSAT "risen by more than 40 points"; "75% of customers now connect on Starlink wifi A350 flights against 10% across the rest of the fleet". The article notes carriers have yet to turn this into revenue figures.

No public United/Hawaiian/Qatar figure ties Starlink to market share, booking share or switching. Hawaiian search yielded only rollout releases.

## C. Key counterpoint: JetBlue already has free Wi-Fi

- Amazon (about Amazon, post Nov 2025): JetBlue "will continue offering complimentary connectivity to all customers with the Kuiper-powered system"; Fly-Fi free since 2013; Kuiper (Amazon Leo) on "select aircraft beginning in 2027."
  https://www.aboutamazon.com/md/news/innovation-at-amazon/jetblue-amazon-project-kuiper-in-flight-wifi-partnership
- Implication: the surveys measure "free/quality Wi-Fi vs none or poor". JetBlue passengers already get free Wi-Fi plus seatback TV, so the Wi-Fi gap vs United is speed/quality, not access. The stated 22-44% figures overstate the JetBlue-to-United delta. (Unverified here: United Starlink is free only to MileagePlus members, which Blue Sky members can join for free.)

## Interpretation for the 10% case
- Stated preference (22%, 29%, 44%, 75%, 83%) is far above 10%, but these are hypothetical and compare Wi-Fi vs no/poor Wi-Fi. Stated-to-revealed haircut is typically large.
- Revealed data (CSAT 2x, usage 10% to 75%) show the product is valued, but no airline has published a share shift from it.
- Net: Wi-Fi is a weak, narrow driver for JBLU to UAL because JetBlue matches on free access and plans a LEO upgrade in 2027. Supports staying at or below 10% from this angle alone; not an argument for going above.
