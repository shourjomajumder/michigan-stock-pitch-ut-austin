# Agent 06: JetBlue and United disclosures on Blue Sky (Jul 2025 to Oct 2026)

Angle: earnings calls, conferences and the FY2025 10-K. Bottom line: neither airline has disclosed a Blue Sky revenue figure, a cannibalization figure, TrueBlue members crediting to United, or share shifts at BOS or NYC. United's calls do not mention JetBlue or Blue Sky at all. The hard numbers below are the only ones on the record.

## Hard numbers (verbatim)

1. Expected value: "The collaboration is expected to contribute an incremental $50 million EBIT through 2027, accelerating Jet Forward." (Joanna Geraghty, JBLU Q2 2025 call, Jul 29, 2025, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jblu/transcripts/340237-q2-2025/). She said this is "largely associated with the benefits from Paisly" and that full run rate comes "until 2028". On United flying the JFK slots: "we've made certain assumptions in our model as to what the impact of United flying those slots may be" and "there is an impact to JetBlue if they operate those slots". The impact was not quantified.
   - Q4 2025 call, Jan 27, 2026: "Blue Sky and Paisly are expected to drive another three-quarters a point" of RASM in 2026 (Marty St. George, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jblu/transcripts/403547-q4-2025/).
   - Q2 2026 call, Jul 28, 2026: "We did put the benefits of the United relationship in the guide when we updated to $850 million-$950 million" (St. George, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jblu/transcripts/656107-q2-2026/).
   - Implication: management treats Blue Sky as net positive, including its own assumption about leakage to United. This points below 10%, but it is a biased source.

2. Award traffic: "has now passed more than 1.1 billion TrueBlue points redeemed on United flights ... since the loyalty assets launched in February" (St. George, JBLU Q3 2026 call, Oct 27, 2026, https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/27/jetblue-jblu-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ ; also https://www.webull.com/news/15821575681355776).
   - Date conflict: the 10-K says "In October 2025, customers became eligible to earn and redeem points across both" airlines. "February" may refer to cross-merchandising.
   - My own rough estimate, not sourced: at about 1.0 to 1.5 cents per point, that is about $11M to $17M of award travel. That would be roughly 0.1 to 0.2% of JetBlue's annual revenue (about $9B).
   - Implication: award-driven switching is far below 10%. This measures redemptions only, not paid switching.

3. Share and overlap bounds:
   - 10-K FY2025 (as of Dec 31, 2025), JetBlue seat share: "New York metropolitan area | 118 [nonstop routes] | 13%" and "Boston | 78 | 26%" (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158463/000115846326000007/jblu-20251231.htm). The NY metro figure includes JFK, EWR, LGA, HPN and ISP.
   - Cirium data, June 2025, New England plus New York: "United is the second largest, with a 17% share, and JetBlue is third, with a nearly 15% share" (The Points Guy, Jun 2025, https://thepointsguy.com/news/spirit-airlines-blue-sky-objection).
   - Overlap, as Spirit claims it in its DOT filing: "18 overlap routes plus another 55 that United flies from Newark which JetBlue flies from JFK" (Cranky Flier, Jun 2025, https://crankyflier.com/?p=42533). Unverified.
   - JFK slots: "JetBlue will provide United access to slots at JFK International Airport for up to seven daily round-trip flights" starting "as early as 2027"; also an "exchange [of] eight flight timings at Newark" (press release, Jul 29, 2025, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250729944822/en/JetBlue-and-United-Complete-DOT-Review-of-Blue-Sky-Collaboration).

4. Upper bound on product-driven switching (United CEO, not about JetBlue):
   - "We have three big hubs where we have three different big competitors," "each of which we've won about 20 points of market share." and "the brand loyal customers are switching." (Scott Kirby, UAL Q1 2026 call, Apr 22, 2026, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/transcripts/544206-q1-2026/)
   - Chicago: "we had a 19-point advantage with local market share and a 38-point advantage with business traffic"; "We won because of product, service, reliability, and technology." (Kirby, JPM conference, Mar 17, 2026, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ual/transcripts/608753-jpmorgan-industrials-conference-2026/)
   - Implication: these gains are multi-year (since around 2019, my reading; no time window was stated), so roughly 3 to 5 points per year. Over 1 to 2 years that is about 5 to 10 points. This is a ceiling consistent with 10% or a bit below, and it applies where United has hub scale. At JFK, United will have at most 7 daily round trips from 2027.

## Qualitative signals (direction: JetBlue says traffic flows to it, not away)
- "The top JetBlue markets booked on united.com are to and from United hubs" (Geraghty, JPM, Mar 17, 2026, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/jblu/transcripts/596147-jpmorgan-industrials-conference-2026/). Interline cross-selling is "tracking better than expected."
- "A TrueBlue customer should have little reason to go outside of the JetBlue United partnership" (same source).
- "We've seen redemptions go both directions ... it is more or less what we'd expected." (St. George, Q3 2025, Oct 28, 2025, https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/10/28/jetblue-jblu-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
- "the whole point is not to give customers any reason to choose anyone but JetBlue, particularly in Boston and New York." (St. George, Q1 2026, Apr 28, 2026, https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/jetblue-jblu-q1-2026-earnings-155409355.html). He also cited "Surprisingly good results at DCA" from MileagePlus customers.
- The partnership "does not include revenue sharing and does not include any sort of coordination" (St. George, Morgan Stanley Laguna, Sep 11, 2025).
- No analyst on any call reviewed asked about cannibalization or JetBlue passengers switching to United. The closest question was Jamie Baker's (JPM, Q2 2025) on United flying the JFK slots.
- United's Q3 2025, Q1 2026, JPM 2026 and Laguna 2026 transcripts contain no mention of JetBlue, Blue Sky, or JFK slots. The Q4 2025 and Q2 2026 UAL transcripts failed to load.
