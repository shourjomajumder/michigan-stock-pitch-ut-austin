# a7_bluesky_now: Blue Sky switching measured so far (event q0 = 2025Q4; latest Table 6 = 2026Q1)
- Data: DOT Table 6 (data.transportation.gov yj5y-b2ir), 2022Q1-2026Q1, 12 treated city pairs (BOS-LAX/SFO/CHI/HOU/DEN, NYC-LAX/LAS/RSW/TPA/PBI/MCO, MIA-SFO; NYC-SJU not in Table 6) vs 14 controls (BOS-MCO/TPA/RSW/PBI/JAX/LAS/BUF/PIT/RDU/SYR/RIC, NYC-BUF/ROC/SYR). Panel: a7_bluesky_now_work/t6.csv; code: did.py.
- Metric: JetBlue share YoY change (same quarter, prior year), treated minus control, route-equal means; SE = sqrt(varT/nT + varC/nC).
- Pre-period (2023Q1-2025Q3) DiD is persistently negative: mean -2.80 pts/yr (SD 2.00, n=11). JetBlue was already losing share faster on United-shared routes before Blue Sky, so the pre-trend fails.
- Post: k=0 (2025Q4) -1.11 (SE 1.37); k=1 (2026Q1) +6.91 (SE 1.88). Pair+quarter FE event study, clustered by pair (25 pairs, 275 obs): k=0 +1.85 (1.51), k=1 +9.86 (2.27). Pooled post +5.79 (1.73). Positive = JetBlue did better on shared routes, the wrong sign for switching.
- Boston only: k=0 -1.17 (1.61), k=1 +4.89 (3.37); pre mean -1.07, so k=0 is about 0 after the trend. NYC only: k=0 +2.20 (2.21), k=1 +10.12 (2.84); NYC controls are just 3 small upstate markets.
- 2026Q1 is driven by controls: JetBlue's share fell about 10 pts YoY on its own leisure/upstate routes (e.g. BOS-TPA 50.8->36.1, BOS-PIT 47.6->37.2, NYC-ROC 30.5->15.9). T-100 Boston seats Q1 2026 YoY: B6 control -8.5%, treated -6.2%; Q4 2025 treated -4.0% vs control +4.8%. So the moves are capacity/shock-driven, not loyalty-driven.
- United share on treated routes (no control possible): YoY +2.12 (SE 0.77) in 2025Q4 and +2.84 (0.69) in 2026Q1, vs +0.9 to +1.7 in 2025Q1-Q3 (before Blue Sky). A small acceleration, about 1 SE, not separable from United's trend.
- T-100 is still at July 2026 (TranStats release info, updated 11/11/2026; July added Oct 9, 2026). Aug/Sep 2026 not published, so no extension of the load-factor test; prior result (July 2026 beta2 +3.75, YoY +0.85, n.s.) stands. DB1B ends 2025Q2.
- Statements after Oct 1 2026: JetBlue Q3 call (Oct 27) said ">1.1 billion TrueBlue points redeemed on United flights since February" and reported "record levels of active TrueBlue members and elite status qualifications". The accessible United Q3 call (Oct 21) coverage has no Blue Sky usage metric; the full transcript is paywalled.
- Timing implication: no measurable switching at k=0-1 (earn/redeem phase). The elite-perk phase (May 2026) has 1 month of data. First possible Table 6 read of the elite phase is 2026Q2/Q3 (not yet released). Consistent with precedents where effects lag more than 4 quarters.
- Caveats: Table 6 shows a carrier's share only if it is the largest or lowest-fare carrier (censoring; n varies 7-10 treated per quarter). City-pair O&D includes connections. CHI never shows B6. 2026Q1 has a JetBlue-wide shock of unknown cause.
