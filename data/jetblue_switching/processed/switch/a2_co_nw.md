# a2_co_nw: CO-NW (1998) and DL-CO-NW (2003) event-time share paths, DOT Table 6 (SODA yj5y-b2ir; feed starts 1996Q1 for these pairs, no 1995)
Event dates (GAO RCED-99-37, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-RCED-99-37/html/GAOREPORTS-RCED-99-37.htm): announced Jan 1998; clubs opened Nov 21 1998; reciprocal earning Dec 6 1998; codeshare bookings Dec 12 1998, flights Jan 7 1999; redemption Feb 1 1999 (travel from Mar 1 1999). k=0 = 1998Q4 (first full quarter 1999Q1); base = 1997Q1-Q4 average (avoids Jan-98 announcement and the Aug 29-Sep 12 1998 NW pilot strike, which shows as k=-1 drops of 11-21 pts on every NW route). DL-CO-NW: reciprocal earning, elite and lounge benefits from Jun 18 2003 (InsideFlyer https://insideflyer.com/2003/06/27/continental-delta-northwest-alliance-starts-up/); redemption later in 2003; k=0 = 2003Q2, base 2002Q2-2003Q1.
Usable CO-NW loser paths (pts vs base): Cleveland-Minneapolis NW (base 69.0): k-2 -7 (post-announcement), k0 -7, k1 -8, k2 -11, k3-k7 -15/-16 (peak), k8-k14 -9 to -12 (9/11 at k11). Houston-Memphis NW (48.0, falling since 1996 = pretrend): k0 -7, k2 -10, k4-k6 -12/-13, k8-k14 -6 to -15. Detroit-Houston CO (45.5; NW the gainer, 0.33-0.36 Q1 pre to 0.40 1999Q1, largest in 2000H1): k1 -2, k4 -4, k7-k14 about -2 (within seasonal noise). Minneapolis-New York NW: k1 -3, k2 -4, then Sun Country entry (1999Q3) dominates.
No effect / not alliance: Cleveland-Detroit NW (76 base; flat, then 83-93 after CO pulled back post-2001). Detroit-New York NW: 1998 dip fully recovered by k4-k5 (back to 0.71-0.72 in 2000); 2001Q3+ decline is Spirit (NK) entry. Cleveland-Memphis NW -14 at k0 to -33 at k5 and about -40 later, but CO became largest (0.47-0.55) from 2000Q2: capacity entry, excluded. Houston-Minneapolis: SY entry hits both partners, excluded.
Timing (CO-NW, 3 usable routes): first effect k0-k1 (CLE-MSP already moved at k-2 after announcement); 50% of eventual shift by k0-k1; 90% by k2-k4; peak k3-k6; then about one-third decay on CLE-MSP (peak -16 to plateau -11) and persistence through 2002-2003 on CLE-MSP and HOU-MEM.
DL-CO-NW 2003: Cincinnati-Houston DL (54.5): k0 -2, k1 -6, k1-k8 mean about -4 (CO low-fare share 0.32-0.36 pre to 0.37-0.42), i.e. 50% by k0, ~90% by k1; later -10/-18 at k9-k10 coincides with DL Ch.11 (Sep 2005) and CVG cuts. Atlanta-Detroit NW: no shift (±4 noise). Atlanta-Minneapolis NW -3 by k4, -7 at k6 and Atlanta-Houston DL -12 by k8: both track AirTran (FL) growth, not attributable.
Best-supported number: Cleveland-Minneapolis, NW lost about 11 share points persistently (69 to ~58; CO 0.16-0.19 to 0.27-0.32), 90% within 2 quarters of reciprocal earning, peak at 3-6 quarters.

EWR); full alliance (codeshare + FF + clubs + equity), not loyalty-only; capacity changes could not be separated (no T-100 pulled), so CO gains on CLE-MSP may partly be added CO flights; n is small (3 + 1 usable of 12 routes).
