# a6 loyalty events: how fast frequent flyers move after loyalty shocks (sources in a6_loyalty_events.csv)
- Stated intent moves at once (lag 0-1 mo): Delta 2023 negative sentiment +352% in week 1; rivals launched matches within ~2 weeks; United said matches were up "dramatically" but "not a big number" at ~1 mo; BA 62% feel worse within 3 mo; WN informal survey 2 of 3 "looking".
- Measured revenue/card loss: none visible at 1, 4 or 15 months for Delta (record 2023 acquisitions, co-brand spend +15%, FY24 loyalty rev +11%) or at ~7 months for Southwest (loyalty rev +7%, card acquisitions up double digits). Two of the shocks (DL in ~1 mo, BA in ~2 mo) were partly reversed, which truncated them.
- Hard churn found in only one case: Cathay 2015 devaluation lost 10.7% of elites over ~1-2 qualification cycles. Volume proxy: ~100K matches away from Delta in H2 2023 (an unverified estimate; a match is not a switch).
- A product/ops shock without a reversal (UA/CO, Mar 2012) showed a revenue gap in quarter 0-1 (PRASM ~3.5 pts below peers) that persisted for ~2 years (no decay by 2014). Ops failures confound it.
- Seasonality: status-match searches spike every January as status expires. Orhun et al.: lock-in peaks near the status threshold (relative share -9.6% to -10.7%), is lowest at cycle start and once the member is well ahead, and resets each cycle.
- Mechanics: Mosaic and Premier both qualify on the calendar year and run to Jan 31 of the year after next. 2026-earned Mosaic is valid to Jan 31 2028. A UA challenge takes 120 days, can be done once per 3 years, and if finished after Jul 1 gives status to Jan 31 two years later.
- Implied rule: elites switch *program* at reset dates, not on the event date. Non-elites and flight choice drift continuously. Under Blue Sky, a Mosaic member can fly UA metal booked on jetblue.com and still earn Mosaic, so metal switching has no status cost and can start now. Program switching (to MileagePlus) clusters at Jan 2027 (2025-earned status lapses) and Jan 2028 (2026-earned lapses).
- Timing guess (inference, not measured): first effect Q2-Q3 2026; about 50% by Q1 2027 (first reset); about 90% by Q1 2028 (second reset). This is broadly consistent with the model's full run-rate in Q3 2027, possibly slightly early. Decay: none if no reversal (UA 2012); full reversal if JetBlue matches the product.
- Caveats: n=6 events, no route-level or monthly match counts anywhere public. Airlines report only qualitative statements. AA Loyalty Points (2022) had no switching data.
