# Agent 6: JFK transcon economy product gap, JetBlue vs United (2027)

Prepared 2026-11-11. Public sources only. "Derived" means my arithmetic from cited figures. "ASSUMPTION" marks anything not in a source.

## Bottom line
For an economy customer on JFK-LAX/SFO in 2027, United's edge is **reliability** and **Wi-Fi quality**, and Blue Sky removes most of the **loyalty cost** of switching. JetBlue keeps a **legroom** edge in core (32" vs United's 30" standard economy) and far more economy seats per flight. Cabin and screens are close to parity. United's JFK aircraft, routes and date are still unannounced.

## 2. Three strongest switching reasons (with numbers)

1. **Reliability.** DOT, Jan-Aug 2026: United 76.1% on-time vs JetBlue 67.3% (8.8 pts; rank 3 vs rank 8). August 2026 cancellations: JetBlue 6.2% vs United 1.0% (6.2x). Even in a calm month (June 2026) JetBlue cancelled 1.5% vs 0.6%. JetBlue also had a system-wide crew-scheduling failure in July 2026 that cut JFK operations by 50% (ALPA).
2. **Loyalty switching cost is nearly gone.** A TrueBlue member booking United on jetblue.com earns 5 points per dollar plus Mosaic tiles, vs a minimum 6x on JetBlue. On an illustrative $370 JetBlue JFK-LAX fare (prior work, DOT Table 1a), that is 1,850 vs 2,220 points: 370 points lost per trip (derived; ignores taxes in the fare). Mosaic members also get free Economy Plus on United.
3. **Faster Wi-Fi on a newer cabin.** United's Coastliner has Starlink, free for MileagePlus members. United targets Starlink on all dual-cabin aircraft by end-2027 (official) and more than 1,000 mainline aircraft by May 2027 (leak, not official). JetBlue stays on Viasat, with Kuiper starting only in 2027 and only on older A321s. Weaker reason: both are free today, so the gap is speed, not price.

## 3. Reasons not to switch (counter-evidence)
- Legroom: JetBlue core 32" vs United standard economy 30" (Coastliner economy pitch unpublished).
- Capacity: Coastliner has 93 standard economy seats vs JetBlue's 144 core, 25 fewer than United's own 757-200 (118). Fewer cheap seats means less room for United to match base fares.
- Frequency: base case in prior work is about 7 United daily pairs (LAX 4, SFO 3); JetBlue runs more on each route.
- Timing: Coastliner already slipped from summer 2026 to early 2027, and launches at EWR first. JFK service date, routes and aircraft are unannounced.
