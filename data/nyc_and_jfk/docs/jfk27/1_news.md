## 3. Takeaways for the model
- Nothing after Oct 21, 2026 adds new hard facts. The post-call stories (OMAAT, InsideFlyer, A2Z) recycle the Nocella line and the unsourced "20". The only new hard fact is the Coastliner: delivered Nov 5 and going to **Newark first** in early 2027. That argues for later or non-Coastliner JFK flying (ASSUMPTION: JFK launch at least one season after EWR Coastliner service begins).
- Use 7 pairs as the base case. Treat 20 as a bull case with no primary source. The leak itself was ambiguous between 20 pairs and 20 departures (10 pairs).
- Watch for: United route announcement or GDS load for JFK (none yet); the UAL Q4 call (~Jan 20-21, 2027); JetBlue's Q4 call (~late Jan 2027).
