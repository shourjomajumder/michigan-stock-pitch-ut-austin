# a01: measured factor vols, re-run of pair betas

Method: kept scen.py's leg correlations, leg vols (pair_daily.csv, 2025-11-04 to 2026-10-27) and hedge 1.163. Swapped the 4 ASSUMED factor vols for sourced realized values. beta_leg = corr x leg vol / factor vol; beta_pair = beta_ULCC - 1.163 x beta_JBLU. Pair beta scales as 1/factor vol, so pair corr and residual vol do not change. Script: a01/rebeta.py. Outputs: a01/factor_betas_a01.csv, a01/pair_macro_scenarios_a01.csv.

Factor vols used (annualized, close-to-close)
- XLY 17.5% (was 20%). Source: optionsanalysissuite.com XLY IV/HV page, HV60 16.3-20.2% for Oct 13-Nov 2 2026; alphaquery 30d HV 27.4% (Nov 9, a recent spike). No early-2026 data reachable, so I also checked it against SPY 13% x the usual 1.2-1.3 ratio. Range 16-20%.
- IWM 19.5% (was 22%). Source: optionsanalysissuite.com IWM monthly pages, HV60 month-end 18.5% (Jan 30, covers Nov-Jan) and 22.9% (Apr, covers Feb-Apr); IV/HV page HV60 14.2-17.4% (Oct 19-Nov 6). May-Sep missing. Range 17-23%.
- HYG 4.5% (was 6.5%). Source: optionsanalysissuite.com HYG monthly pages, HV60 close 3.0% (Jan 30), 4.8% (Mar 31), 5.1% (Apr 21); HV20 peaked at 7.7% (Mar 31); alphaquery 30d HV 4.55% (Nov 9). May-Sep missing. Range 3.5-5.5%.
- 10y UST 60 bp/yr, about 3.8 bp/day (was 95). Not directly measured: FRED blocked (curl 403, WebFetch got no 2025-26 rows). Inferred from ranges using range ~1.6 sigma sqrt(T): Bloomberg (Jan 16 2026), median weekly range since 2006 16bp, under 10bp for the prior 5 weeks; MUFG (Feb 12 2026), 2025 annual range 84bp. Those imply about 45-70 bp/yr. Range 45-75.

Caveats
- The direction never changes. Every pair beta is a small negative number, so the pair is a mild short on macro risk and gains in sell-offs. These gains are all inside the residual 1sd (18-31%).
- The HYG change is the biggest. A lower measured HYG vol makes the same -5% shock a 3.8-sigma monthly move (it was 2.7-sigma), so a linear beta probably overstates the +7.7%. Read the HYG scenario as a ~4-sigma credit event, not a moderate one. The JBLU leg's credit sensitivity (Oct 2026 liquidity scare) is the real driver.
- The vols are windowed HV60/HV30 snapshots, not one full-year series, and May-Sep 2026 is missing for IWM and HYG. Correlations come from prior work and were not recomputed.
- The 10y vol is inferred from ranges, not from FRED levels. It doesn't matter in practice, because the pair beta to rates is about 0 either way.
