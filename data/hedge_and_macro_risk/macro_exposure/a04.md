Fundamentals (sourced):
- 2Q25 (the tariff demand dip): Frontier TRASM fell 2% YoY to 9.01c and load factor rose 1.2 pts to 79.3%, on ASMs down 2% and passengers down 4%. Frontier said April domestic demand dropped, then stabilized later in the quarter (Frontier 8-K, SEC). JetBlue RASM fell 1.5% to 14.17c and load factor fell 2.1 pts to 81.9%, on ASMs down 1.5% (JetBlue release, Business Wire, 7/29/25). Unit revenue declines were about the same, so there is no clear trade-down edge either way.
- 2009: JetBlue made a net profit of $58m (8.5% operating margin), its best since 2003. Allegiant's operating margin was 21.9% (Aviation Strategy, Mar 2010). Low-cost carriers held up through the GFC.
- 2025 shock headlines: on 4/3/25 ULCC and JBLU both fell 11% (Motley Fool). Frontier pulled its 2025 guidance.

Read-across:
1. Outright demand or panic shocks (Mar 2020, Feb-Apr 2025): the ULCC-type long falls more, and the pair loses about 3-6%. The high-beta ULCC equity, and its operating leverage on the $1 long, beat the 1.163x hedge. With ALGT as proxy, 2020 is +14%, so the sign depends on balance sheet (SAVE and ULCC are levered, ALGT was not).
2. Slower recessions and rate bears (2008-09, 2022): the pair gained +24% to +56%. Most of this is JBLU-specific: 2022 Spirit bid and NEA unwind, and in 2008-09 ALGT was a uniquely strong proxy. Do not treat it as clean macro beta.
3. Net: the downturn exposure has an uncertain sign and is roughly neutral to slightly short market. In the first 1-3 months of a sharp consumer shock, the pair has historically been about -3% to -6% (net long). Over a full recession it has been positive, but driven by idiosyncratic factors. This agrees with scen.py: SPY -20% gives +4.7%, with a 1-sd range of +/-31% that swamps it.

Caveats:
- Proxies (ALGT, SAVE) are not ULCC.
- Highs and lows are on unaligned dates.
- Oil is included in the 2008 and 2022 moves (both were fuel spikes).
- Calendar-year windows are not shock windows.
- 2020 and 2022 RASM and load factor were not collected, for lack of time.
- The macrotrends ULCC 2025 row looked stale, so I used the MarketBeat range instead. The $2.79 low is assumed to be Apr 2025, since the 2024 low was $2.84.
