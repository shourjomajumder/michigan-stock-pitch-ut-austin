# a03: Hedge-ratio stability, pair vol, VaR/ES, min-variance hedge

Source for every number: pair_daily.csv (daily closes, 250 simple returns, 2025-11-04 to 2026-11-02). Script: a03/a03.py. Outputs: a03/rolling_series.csv, a03/rolling_hedge_ratio.png.

Method: pair return = r_ULCC - 1.163*r_JBLU per $1 long ULCC (static hedge, no oil leg). Rolling OLS beta = cov/var of ULCC on JBLU. VaR/ES are historical (empirical quantile / tail mean); 21-day uses overlapping 21-day sums of daily pair P&L (230 windows). Max DD on cumulative static P&L.

Read: on returns, 1.163 over-hedges. ULCC moves only ~0.8x JBLU, so the pair is net short about 0.37 units of JBLU beta (1.163 - 0.79). That means it is net short the shared airline/market factor, which fits the negative pair betas in factor_betas.csv (SPY -0.24, HYG -1.06). The hedge removes only 36% of ULCC variance (47% at min-var), so the pair is still mostly idiosyncratic risk at about 60% annual vol.

Caveats
- One year of data (~250 obs); 60d betas have wide standard errors. 21-day VaR has only ~11 independent windows, so its tails are unreliable. Square-root scaling is shown for comparison.
- 21d < sqrt-21 scaling suggests some mean reversion in pair P&L over this sample; don't rely on it.
- 1.163 may be a dollar or EV-sizing choice rather than a return-beta hedge. If the goal is dollar-neutral factor exposure, the data supports ~0.8.
- Simple returns, no dividends/borrow costs; no oil leg (per brief).
