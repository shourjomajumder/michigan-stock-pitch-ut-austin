Coverage: 647 rows. Core series = MIT Airline Data Project (DOT Form 41 T2), 2005-2020: dom_rpm_mn (mainline domestic RPMs, millions), dom_share_rpm_pct, and dom_yield_cents_per_rpm for AA, DL, UA, WN, B6, AS, HA, G4, F9, NK, VX (VX RPM only, 2007-2018). No Sun Country in MIT data.
dom_share_rpm_pct is COMPUTED by me = carrier mainline domestic RPM / MIT "Total Industry" domestic RPM (the total includes regionals, so mainline shares are understated for AA/DL/UA, which fly a lot through regional partners; merger years like DL 2010, UA 2012, AA 2015 show jumps). The underlying RPMs appear on the page.
2021-2025 gap: no per-carrier domestic share by year was found. Stopgaps: Feb-2024 trailing-12-month RPM share for DL/AA/WN/UA (aeroxplorer article, metric dom_share_rpm_pct_ttm_feb2024, year=2023); BTS transtats AA share TTM Jul 2025/2026; DOT Q4-2024 Quarterly Financial Review SYSTEM RPMs and yield 2023-24 (sys_rpm_bn, sys_yield_cents, not domestic only; UA/NK/SY/WN-yield missing); Wikipedia SYSTEM total passengers 2019-2025 incl. SY (sys_pax_total; DL figures are rounded); BTS 2017-18 domestic enplanements (dom_pax_mn).
avg_fare_usd: not found by carrier (BTS fare releases are by airport only). Use dom_yield_cents_per_rpm as the price proxy instead.
Caveats: the MIT Virgin America yield row did not line up with its years, so I dropped it. The Northwest/Continental/US Airways/AirTran legacy rows were skipped. 2020 is a COVID outlier.
