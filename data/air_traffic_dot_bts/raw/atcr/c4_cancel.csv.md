- Coverage: cancel_pct (mainline/operating carrier, DOT ATCR Tables 6B/6C, full year) is complete for 10 carriers (AA, DL, UA, WN, AS, B6, NK, F9, HA, G4) for 2021-2025. 2020 is partial (8 carriers, no AA/UA mainline); 2019 has only G4 and WN, from DOT's list of top 3 and bottom 3 carriers. cancel_pct_network = marketing carrier including branded regional partners (AA/DL/UA/AS 2021-25, plus partial 2019-20).
- complaints_per_100k (DOT complaints per 100,000 passengers, as compiled in the Wichita State/Embry-Riddle Airline Quality Rating): 2009-2010 and 2015-2022 for most carriers; VX 2015-16; G4 only from 2019. 2023 is partial (6 carriers, from PIRG).
- Gaps: no carrier-level annual cancellations before 2019, because the annual ATCR tables (6B/6C) only start about 2018 and WebFetch cut off the older PDFs; BTS TranStats and the DOT index pages are blocked here. Sun Country is never in the ATCR cancellation tables and has no complaint values. Complaints are missing for 2005-2008, 2011-2014 and 2024-2025 (the AQR PDFs for those years did not load). Since 2023 DOT publishes complaints as separate "consumer submission data".
- Caveats: the 2019-2020 press-release values are rounded to 1 decimal. The 2020 rates reflect COVID schedule cuts (e.g., G4 14.5%). The 2020 complaints spike comes from refund complaints (F9 49.3, HA 37.6, UA 29.7), so treat 2020 as an outlier year.
- Mainline vs network: AA, DL, UA and AS mainline rates differ from their network rates (e.g., AA 2025: 1.82 mainline vs 2.36 network), so pick one consistently. Single-brand carriers have the same value under both.
