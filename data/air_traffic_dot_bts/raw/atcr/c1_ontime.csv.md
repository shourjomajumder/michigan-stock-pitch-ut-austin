- Coverage: 169 rows, annual on-time arrival % (DOT Air Travel Consumer Report, Feb issue = Jan-Dec totals) for 2006, 2008, 2010-2025; AA, DL, UA, WN, AS, B6, F9, HA every covered year; NK 2015+, G4 2018+, VX 2012-2017.
- Basis: own-metal operating-carrier figures (ATCR Table 1A "12 months ending Dec" pre-2018; Table 1D / mainline sub-rows 2018-2025), which exclude regional partners. Exception: 2019 uses Table 1B marketing-carrier YTD, so AA/DL/UA/AS that year include branded regional codeshares (not strictly comparable).
- Gaps: 2005, 2007, 2009 missing (ATCR PDFs not reachable or no 12-month column; the BTS annual-rankings xlsx at bts.gov was blocked by the proxy). Sun Country (SY) never appears as a DOT reporting carrier in fetched tables. Spirit not a reporting carrier before 2015, Allegiant before 2018.
- Caveats: 2014 AA = combined AA+US Airways (AA-only 75.8) and WN = combined WN+AirTran (WN-only 73.0). Pre-2018 values are 1-decimal; 2020+ are 2-decimal. 2020 is a COVID outlier (light schedules, high OTP).
- Values were extracted from PDFs via a fetch/summarize tool; worth spot-checking a few against the BTS file Annual-Airline-On-Time-Rankings-2003-2025 xlsx if you can download it, which would also fill 2005/2007/2009.
