# a10_entry_ramp: how fast an entrant's route share ramps (DOT Table 6, quarterly; script (orig workspace) switch/a10/a10_calc.py)
- n=8 entrant paths (Delta SEA: GEG, BOI, EUG, PDX; Delta BOS: PIT, BUF; Southwest DEN 2006: PHX, LAS) + incumbent loss paths on 6 (GEG, BOI, EUG, PDX, BUF, DEN-CHI). k=0 = first quarter entrant is visible / service starts.
- Median entrant share as fraction of mature (last 4 pre-COVID qtrs), 3q-smoothed, k=0..12: .49 .54 .61 .63 .70 .80 .87 .88 .87 .78 .82 .91 .95.
- Quarters to 50% of mature: median 0-1 (0,0,0,0,1,1,7,8). To 90%: median ~6 (EUG 3, BOI 4, BUF 5, PIT 6; LAS 11, PHX 12, GEG 16, PDX 18).
- Two regimes: step entry with full schedule day one (BOI, EUG, BUF, PIT) -> half of mature share in q0, 90% in 3-6 qtrs; gradual capacity builds (GEG, PDX, WN at DEN) -> roughly linear, 90% only after 11-18 qtrs. Speed tracks seats, not loyalty.
- Peak/decay: BOI peaked ~k8-9 (DL ~28%, AS 67.6% low) then gave back ~5pt by 2019 (AS 73->77); EUG peaked k7-8 (~32%) then ~29%. Others plateau. Overshoot ~15-20% of peak where it happens.
- Incumbent loss: median ~50% of eventual loss by k2-4, ~100% by k6 (noisy). Incumbent bears entrant gain 1:1 only if it was a monopoly (SEA). With other carriers present it bears less: BOS-BUF B6 -10.9pt vs DL +28.9 (~38%); BOS-PIT B6 -1.6pt vs DL +31 (~5%, DL took AA/US share). DEN-CHI UA -11pt by k6 vs WN ~18-19.
- United JFK: UA re-entered JFK 28 Mar 2021 (JFK-SFO/LAX), left 29 Oct 2022; no 2023 return (brief's "2023" appears wrong); 2027 return via JetBlue slot deal (announced May 2025). Table 6 is metro-level (EWR+JFK+LGA): NYC-SFO UA 33.7% (2019 avg) vs 31.9-37.3% in 2021Q2-22Q3 = no visible re-entry effect; after exit YoY -0.1, -3.1, -4.8, -1.9pt (2023Q1-Q4). NYC-LAX inconclusive (UA/B6 swap largest slot at 26-29%).
- Not covered: Southwest Hawaii (Table 6 excludes HI), JetBlue LAX/FLL and Southwest BOS/EWR (metro markets already contain entrant's other airports: LGB, FLL in Miami mkt, PVD/MHT).
- Caveats: Table 6 shows only largest and lowest-fare carrier; entrant visible only when cheapest (upward-biased, gappy) except on ex-monopoly routes where entrant = incumbent loss. Passenger shares, not seats; T-100 seats not pulled. COVID truncates BOS/EUG at 8-11 qtrs. Event quarter is data-defined (first appearance).
- Implication for UA at JFK 2027 vs B6: share should show ~half its eventual level in the first quarter and ~90% within ~1.5 years if UA launches a full schedule; ramp is slower only if UA adds frequencies gradually.
