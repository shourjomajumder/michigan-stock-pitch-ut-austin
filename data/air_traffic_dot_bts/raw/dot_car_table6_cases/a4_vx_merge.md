# a4_vx_merge: Alaska/Virgin America (close 2016Q4; Elevate fold + single certificate 2018Q1 = main event k0; brand retired 2019Q2). DOT Table 6 (SODA yj5y-b2ir), 9 city pairs, 2015Q1-2020Q4; 2020 excluded (COVID).
- No broad loyalist defection. VX/AS share held or rose after the Elevate fold: NYC-SFO 17.8 (VX, 2016) -> 19.4-20.7 (AS, 2018Q2-Q4) -> 19.2-21.5 (2019); LAX-SFO AS 11.2 -> 14.7; LAS-SFO 15.9/15.7 (VX 2017Q3-Q4) -> 16.2/17.7 (AS 2018) -> 18.4 (2019Q4). Losses were small: LAX-NYC 15.9 (2016) -> 14.3 (2017), then flat around 14.2 after the fold (the decline came after the close, not after the loyalty fold); BOS-SFO 12.3 -> 10.3-11.9 (-1 pt).
- Rival gains after the fold (k = quarters after 2018Q1; change vs the 2017 4Q average): UA SFO-WAS -2.7/-4.9 at k1-2, then +3.0/+3.6/+3.0 at k3-5, +5.6 at k7 (same-quarter YoY +2.6 to +3.7 through 2019). UA NYC-SFO flat at k0-4 (-1.2 to +0.8), then a step to +2.8/+2.6/+2.3 at k5-7 (this coincides with the 2019Q2 brand retirement). UA LAX-NYC +0.4/+1.8/+2.0 at k1-3, +1.5 to +2.7 at k5-7, but only about +0.5 vs 2016 (recovery from a 2017 dip).
- Timing (rival-gain series, n=3): first effect k2-k5 (median about k3); 50% at k2-k5; 90% at k3-k5. Effects are step-like, not gradual ramps. Peak at k5-k7; no decay before COVID (window ends k7). Reaction to the brand retirement (E3) on NYC-SFO was immediate (k0).
- Non-loyalty moves dominate. UA CHI-SFO +7.4 YoY at the close quarter (2017Q1), the same quarter VX dropped out of view, likely capacity. B6 BOS-SFO 22.9 -> 35.2 (2018Q1) -> 35.1 avg (2019), and B6 BOS-LAX 22.3 -> 33-36. These track JetBlue capacity (Mint), began before the fold, and dwarf any VX loss. WN LAX-SFO dipped 3-4 pts in 2018Q2-Q3 as AS added flights, recovered by 2019. AS SFO-SEA flat around 54 after the fold, with WN steady at about 18.
- Caveats: Table 6 shows only the largest and lowest-fare carriers (VX/AS and rivals are often invisible). Pairs are metro city pairs, so OAK/SJC/BUR/LGB/EWR/IAD/DCA are pooled. 2018Q1 VX and AS were still reported separately (NYC-SFO VX 12.8 understates the merged share). No capacity data were pulled, so capacity vs loyalty is separated only by timing and judgment.
- Best-supported number: about 0 net loss of merged-carrier share on overlap routes from removing the loyalty program; rivals' gains, where real, were about 2.5-3.5 pts and arrived 3-5 quarters after the loyalty fold.
