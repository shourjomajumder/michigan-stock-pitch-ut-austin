# Michigan Stock Pitch UT Austin

The data and calculations behind our pair-trade pitch: long Frontier Group Holdings (ULCC), short JetBlue Airways (JBLU), hedged at 1.16 shares of JBLU per share of ULCC. `data/` holds the data by topic (flights and fares, air traffic from DOT/BTS, stock prices, hedge and macro risk, SEC filings, Frontier routes and load factor, JetBlue switching cases, New York and JFK, company and industry facts, valuation inputs), each with a SOURCES file, and `code/` holds the scripts used for the calculations, in matching folders. `DATA_INDEX.md` lists every file. Bloomberg data, broker reports and the Excel valuation models are not included. Commit dates are the dates the data was pulled, taken from the original files.
